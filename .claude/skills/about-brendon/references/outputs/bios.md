# Output guide: bios and summaries

Covers standalone bios (short, long, third-person), LinkedIn "About" sections, resume/CV summaries, speaker and author bios, podcast intros, and cover-letter openers. Start from the Short Bio and Long Bio in the profile; adapt length, person, and emphasis to the venue.

## Length presets

| Venue | Words | Person | Lead with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speaker/author bio | 50–75 | third | title, Architecture team scope, one headline project |
| Social profile bio (X, GitHub bio field) | ≤160 chars | first | title + the one thing he does ("Architecture, APIs, and AI enablement for .NET + React teams") |
| LinkedIn About | 150–250 | first | current scope → origin story in one sentence → what he's open to |
| Resume summary | 40–70 | none (fragment style) | years, stack, modernization + AI leadership, team leadership |
| Cover letter opener | 80–120 | first | the specific match between his modernization/AI work and the role |
| Podcast/conference intro | 40–60, spoken | third | something a host can read aloud; include the origin story hook |

## Rules of thumb

- **One memorable specific per bio.** Candidates: the "super boring" release, no caching needed in any API, 40+ engineers trained, monolith → team-owned .NET 10 APIs, Communication Studies degree → Lead Staff Engineer.
- **Numbers only when the profile has them.** 40+ engineers, six product teams, seven years at FMG, 90%/80% performance gains at ProAg/Epicor. Don't round up or estimate new ones.
- **Don't stack titles.** "Lead Staff Software Engineer" is enough; don't add "technical leader and architect."
- **For hiring-manager audiences**, close with what he's open to (Staff/Principal roles, or project contracting) only if the venue allows it. Not in a speaker bio.
- **Resume summaries** stay in fragment style with no "I": "Lead Staff Software Engineer with 15 years in software, 7 at FMG Suite leading architecture, .NET modernization, and AI adoption across a 40+ engineer department."

## Example: 60-word third-person speaker bio

Brendon Otto is a Lead Staff Software Engineer on the Architecture team at FMG Suite, where he leads the decomposition of a .NET monolith into team-owned APIs and the company's AI adoption across more than forty engineers. He came to software through a Communication Studies degree, an installation-tech job, and a habit of taking systems apart to understand them.
