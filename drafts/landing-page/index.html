More about me

I'm a Lead Staff Software Engineer at FMG Suite, where I've spent seven years moving from senior engineer on the website team, to team lead, to co-founding the company's Architecture team. Today that team guides the flagship product's architectural decisions and builds the foundations other product teams ship on.

My path into software was not a computer science degree. After a Communication Studies degree from the University of Minnesota and a stint in insurance sales, I took an installation technician job at Insite Software just to get inside a technology company. Doing Tier 2 and 3 support taught me how the whole system fit together, and volunteering for small customizations in Progress turned into a programming job earned by delivering projects on time and under budget. When the product was rebuilt in C#, I taught myself at night and designed the bridges between the two systems. That habit of getting into a system, breaking it down to its pieces, and making sense of it carried me through Epicor, ProAg Crop Insurance, and into FMG.

At FMG I ran the original React proofs of concept that replaced Knockout.js, chose Material UI as the component foundation, and later led the team that shipped Visual Editing, a large product and process change that a shortened timeline did not derail. The VP of Engineering called the release "super boring," which was the point. I started the Content API, helped lay the foundations for the Management API, built the Site API, and set the conventions behind them: RESTful design, resources and actions identified early, and performance tuned from day one so caching is a last resort. None of those APIs has needed a data cache yet.

For the last two years I've led FMG's AI adoption: day-and-a-half Copilot workshops for every product team, weekly department trainings, layered AGENTS.md conventions across repositories, and engineering standards developed with the VP so the company can measure the return on its AI investment. My current project, Montage, decouples the ASP.NET monolith into .NET 10 containerized APIs that each team owns and releases independently.

I believe software is a team sport, that performance is a feature, and that once a decision is made everyone gets in line. Outside work I 3D print, do woodworking, train Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, and walk my dog Oliver.
