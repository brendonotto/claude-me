# Hi, I'm Brendon 👋

I'm a Lead Staff Software Engineer at [FMG Suite](https://fmgsuite.com/), on the Architecture team, working remotely from Minnesota. We guide the architectural decisions for FMG's flagship product and build the foundations the other product teams ship on.

## What I do now

- **Montage.** Leading the decoupling of our ASP.NET monolith into .NET 10 containerized APIs, each owned and released by its own team. No more shared central release train.
- **AI adoption across a 40+ engineer department.** Day-and-a-half GitHub Copilot workshops for six product teams, weekly department trainings, layered AGENTS.md conventions across repositories, and custom agents and skills that encode our products for AI-assisted review. Piloted the GitHub Copilot Upgrade Agent for .NET modernization with Microsoft.
- **Engineering standards.** Working with engineering leadership on org-wide standards tied to the return on our AI investment. Eliminate process before automating it.
- **API platform.** Started the Content API, helped lay the foundations for the Management API, built the Site API, and set the conventions behind them: RESTful design, resources and actions identified early, performance tuned from day one. None of them has needed a data cache yet.
- **Front-end platform.** Ran the React proof of concept that replaced Knockout.js and chose Material UI as the component foundation, so engineers aren't making UX decisions ad hoc.

## How I got here

I didn't come in through a computer science degree. After a Communication Studies degree from the University of Minnesota and a stint in insurance sales, I took an installation technician job at Insite Software just to get inside a technology company. Tier 2 and 3 support taught me how the whole system fit together, volunteering for small Progress customizations turned into a programming job, and when the product was rebuilt in C# I taught myself at night. Getting into a system, breaking it down to its pieces, and making sense of it carried me through Epicor and ProAg Crop Insurance and into FMG.

## How I work

- **Software is a team sport.** I do my best work in low-ego environments where nobody cares whose idea it was.
- **Performance is a feature.** Working well means working fast. Tune first; caching needs approval.
- **Disagree, decide, get in line.** Argue the technical case fully, then commit to the decision. No relitigating it afterward, on either side.
- **Eliminate process before automating it.** I don't want to spend tokens on something we shouldn't be doing in the first place.
- **Create over consume.** Shipping code teaches faster than reading about it.
