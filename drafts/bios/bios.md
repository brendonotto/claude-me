## LinkedIn About (first person) — 220 words

I'm a Lead Staff Software Engineer on the Architecture team at FMG Suite. In seven years here I've gone from senior engineer to team lead to co-founding the team that guides our flagship product's architecture. Today that means Montage, decoupling our ASP.NET monolith into .NET 10 containerized APIs each team owns and releases on its own, and leading AI adoption across a 40+ engineer department through per-team workshops and layered AGENTS.md conventions.

Before that I started the Content API, built the Site API, and set the conventions behind them: RESTful design, resources identified early, performance tuned from day one so caching is a last resort. None has needed a data cache yet. I also led the team that shipped Visual Editing on a shortened timeline; the VP of Engineering called the release "super boring," which was the point.

I didn't come in through a CS degree. I have one in Communication Studies, left insurance sales for an installation-tech job, taught myself Progress and then C#, and earned the engineer role by shipping. I still use that degree daily, translating between engineers, product owners, and executives.

Software is a team sport, and performance is a feature. I'm open to Staff or Principal roles and to contract work with CTOs modernizing .NET and React systems or getting their teams productive with AI.
